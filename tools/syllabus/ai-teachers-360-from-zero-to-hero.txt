AI TEACHER 360º

Transform Your Teaching with Ethical Artificial Intelligence

Teach Smarter. Create More. Stay Human.

Artificial Intelligence is changing education — but great teaching will always need great teachers.

AI Teacher 360º is a practical, hands-on course designed to help educators understand, explore and integrate Artificial Intelligence into their professional practice with confidence, creativity and responsibility.

This is not a course about becoming an AI expert.

It is about becoming a teacher who knows when, why and how to use AI effectively — while keeping professional judgement, creativity, critical thinking and human relationships at the centre of education.

⸻

WHY THIS COURSE?

AI tools are developing incredibly fast.

Teachers are already using them. Students are using them. And schools are beginning to ask important questions about what Artificial Intelligence means for teaching and learning.

But using AI effectively requires much more than knowing how to open ChatGPT and write a prompt.

Educators need to understand:

* What AI can and cannot do
* How to communicate effectively with AI tools
* How AI can reduce repetitive workload
* How it can support lesson planning and resource creation
* How it can help differentiate learning
* How to evaluate AI-generated information critically
* How to protect privacy and use AI responsibly
* How to prepare students for an AI-rich world
* And, above all, when not to use AI

AI Teacher 360º gives educators the opportunity to experiment, create, question and build their own responsible approach to Artificial Intelligence.

⸻

THE 360º APPROACH

AI Teacher 360º explores Artificial Intelligence from five complementary perspectives:

01 · UNDERSTAND

Understand how generative AI works, what it can do and where its limitations lie.

No complicated technical explanations.

Just the knowledge educators need to make informed decisions.

02 · CREATE

Use AI as a creative partner to develop:

* Learning activities
* Teaching resources
* Stories and scenarios
* Questions
* Visual concepts
* Projects
* Classroom challenges
* Adapted materials

Move from:

“Generate a worksheet.”

to:

“Help me design a meaningful learning experience.”

03 · PERSONALISE

Explore how AI can support different learners without replacing professional teacher judgement.

Work with:

* Differentiation
* Scaffolding
* Different levels of complexity
* Alternative explanations
* Accessibility
* Student support
* Different learning needs

04 · SAVE TIME

Identify repetitive professional tasks where AI can genuinely help.

Explore practical workflows for:

* Planning
* Brainstorming
* Creating resources
* Preparing communications
* Generating first drafts
* Organising information
* Designing assessment materials
* Supporting feedback

The goal is not to do more work.

It is to create more time for the work that actually needs a teacher.

05 · STAY HUMAN

Explore the questions every educator needs to consider:

* Bias
* Hallucinations
* Reliability
* Privacy
* Data protection
* Academic integrity
* Student dependency
* Critical thinking
* Responsible AI use
* The changing role of the teacher

Because the most important question is not:

“What can AI do?”

It is:

“What should we ask AI to do — and what should remain human?”

5 DAYS TO BECOME AN AI-CONFIDENT TEACHER

DAY 1 · UNDERSTAND IT

AI without the confusion

Participants explore what generative AI actually is and learn through experimentation rather than technical theory.

We explore:

* What Artificial Intelligence is
* How generative AI works
* Opportunities and limitations
* Different types of AI tools
* Hallucinations and reliability
* AI myths
* First practical challenges

Daily challenge:
Test AI. Question it. Break it. Understand it.

⸻

DAY 2 · TALK TO IT

From basic prompts to better conversations

Good AI use starts with knowing how to communicate what you need.

Participants learn how to move beyond simple prompts and develop strategies for getting more useful, relevant and educationally appropriate results.

We explore:

* Anatomy of an effective prompt
* Context
* Roles
* Constraints
* Examples
* Iteration
* Follow-up prompting
* Evaluating and improving AI outputs

Daily challenge:
Turn a weak prompt into a powerful one.

⸻

DAY 3 · CREATE WITH IT

AI as your creative teaching partner

Now AI enters the classroom planning process.

Participants use AI to create and redesign real educational materials and learning experiences.

We explore:

* Lesson planning
* Learning activities
* Stories and scenarios
* Project ideas
* Questions and challenges
* Differentiated resources
* Creative classroom materials
* Assessment ideas
* Visual and multimedia possibilities

Daily challenge:
Create something you could actually use with your students.

⸻

DAY 4 · TEACH WITH IT

Students, learning and responsible AI

The focus moves from the teacher to the learner.

How should students use AI?

When should they use it?

When shouldn’t they?

And what new skills do they need?

We explore:

* Student AI literacy
* Critical evaluation of AI-generated information
* Academic integrity
* Responsible classroom use
* AI dependency
* Maintaining student thinking
* Bias and misinformation
* Privacy and digital responsibility
* Designing meaningful AI-supported learning

Daily challenge:
Design an activity where AI supports thinking instead of replacing it.

⸻

DAY 5 · MAKE IT YOURS

Build your personal AI teaching system

Participants bring everything together and decide how Artificial Intelligence will — and will not — become part of their own professional practice.

We explore:

* Selecting useful tools
* Building personal AI workflows
* Creating reusable prompt systems
* Establishing boundaries
* Developing classroom AI principles
* Planning implementation
* Sharing practices with other educators

Final challenge:

MY AI TEACHER 360º PLAN

Each participant creates a realistic personal implementation plan to take back to their school.
